Kosher Browsing Privacy Policy
Developer: sdmde
Effective: October 20, 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Kosher Browsing, an Android browser developed by sdmde, handles information. It applies to the app identified as org.shield.browser and to this privacy-policy webpage. Contact sdmde at sdmde99@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Kosher Browsing has no developer-operated browsing-history server, account system or cloud-sync service. The app does not include an advertising network or developer analytics SDK, and sdmde does not sell browsing information or share it for targeted advertising. Browsing still involves sending information to the websites and services you choose to use, as explained below.


2. Information stored on your device

The browser stores information in Android app-private storage and its persistent Mozilla Gecko profile to provide normal browsing and recovery. This can include:

• Browsing history: visited website addresses, page titles and visit times, with up to 500 recent history entries.

• Tabs and recovery: open-tab addresses, titles, desktop-site choices and Gecko-provided navigation/session restoration data; the last 20 recently closed tab records may also be retained. Session data may include page-restoration state supplied by the engine.

• Bookmarks: website addresses, titles and folders that you save or import.

• Website data: cookies, website sign-in sessions, cache, local storage and stored site-permission choices. Websites can write their own data to the browser profile.

• Downloads: source URLs, filenames, file types, destination locations, transfer status, progress and times. A website-provided download URL may contain identifiers or access tokens. Downloaded files and partial files are stored in the location used for the download.

• Preferences and protection statistics: theme, layout and accessibility preferences, plus aggregate counts of blocked requests and filtered or failed images. The protection counters do not contain a separate list of browsing URLs.

• Tab previews: page preview images held in memory; the app does not save these preview images as a separate disk archive.


3. Image filtering and browsing protections

The packaged protection extension inspects request addresses and supported search terms locally to apply adult-site, social-site, advertising, Safe Search and YouTube Shorts rules. Image decoding, skin-color detection, grayscale conversion, blur and the enlarged filtering border take place on your device. Network image data is received from the website as part of browsing; inline data/blob images are processed locally before display.

The app does not send image pixels, search terms, browsing history or protection counters to an sdmde server for filtering or classification. Filtering uses a local color rule rather than an external AI or image-analysis service. Bundled app rule lists are snapshots. The underlying browser engine has separate operational and security services, described in the next section.


4. Information sent during browsing

When you open a website, submit a form, sign in, search or download a file, the relevant website and its service providers receive the information needed for that interaction. This can include your IP address, requested address and query, browser/device and language information, cookies, referring-page information where applicable, and information you enter or choose to send. HTTPS encrypts traffic in transit to the destination; ordinary HTTP connections are not encrypted unless they are successfully upgraded.

Address-bar searches use DuckDuckGo. Gmail and Google sign-in are operated by Google. Those providers process the queries, account details and other information you submit to them under their own policies. The browser does not operate an independent password database. Android autofill, if you use it, is handled with your chosen password-manager provider and the website; persistent cookies can keep you signed in.

The embedded Mozilla Gecko engine and Android networking components may make operational requests for DNS resolution, security or certificate checks, and browser security/configuration data. Depending on the feature and provider, those services can receive IP/network metadata, browser information and domain-, URL- or security-list-derived information needed for their function. Providers can include Mozilla, the network or DNS provider in use, and website/security service providers. These engine services are separate from the app’s packaged filtering rules.

The app does not configure its own automatic crash-upload service. The embedded Gecko build disables unified telemetry by default. This does not mean that websites, Android, Google Play or every engine/network service makes no technical or diagnostic requests. Their processing is governed by their respective policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Mozilla Privacy Notices: https://www.mozilla.org/privacy/

5. Actions you choose to share

Sharing a page sends its title and/or URL to the application you select through Android’s share chooser. Copying a link places it on the Android clipboard. Exporting bookmarks writes the saved URLs, titles and folder names to the file or document provider you select. Opening a downloaded file makes it available to the application you choose, subject to Android’s permission controls.

The selected applications and file providers handle the information under their own policies. No page, bookmark export or support email is automatically sent to sdmde through these features.


6. Android permissions and downloads

The app uses internet/network access for browsing, Android notification permission for download progress, and wake-lock capabilities for user-requested downloads while the browser is visible. The embedded browser can adjust audio playback settings. System document pickers provide access to files you select for bookmark import/export or downloads.

This build does not request Android contacts, SMS, camera, microphone or precise-location permissions. Browser and website storage permissions are separate from Android app permissions. You can manage the app’s permissions and notifications in Android Settings.


7. Retention and deleting information

Local browser information remains until it is replaced by normal browser limits, expires under a website’s storage rules, or you remove it. In the app, open Settings → Delete browsing data to choose history, tabs, cookies/site data, cache, stored site permissions and completed download-history entries. Clearing cookies/site data signs you out of websites. Open tabs can retain their navigation history until closed.

Delete bookmarks separately through the Bookmarks screen. Aggregate protection counters and preferences are kept until Android app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Paused or interrupted download records are retained for resumption; clearing all Android app storage removes those records. Files saved to Downloads or another chosen provider, including partial files or bookmark exports, may remain after uninstalling or clearing app data and must be deleted from that location separately.

The app disables Android’s ordinary application backup option, but information you export, download or place with another provider follows that provider’s retention and backup practices. Removing local browser data does not delete information already held by websites, search engines, your email provider or another app you chose to use. Request deletion from those providers directly.


8. Support correspondence and privacy requests

If you email sdmde, the email provider and sdmde receive the sender address, message and any attachments you choose to include. Support correspondence is used to answer the request, troubleshoot the issue and handle related privacy or legal obligations. Do not send passwords, private signing keys, account recovery codes or unnecessary browsing details.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to respond, resolve related requests or meet applicable obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of information you provided directly to sdmde, email sdmde99@gmail.com. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase browser information stored only on your device; use the controls above.


9. This privacy-policy webpage

This policy is provided as a public static webpage without a login, contact form, advertising script or analytics script added by sdmde. Its hosting/platform providers, including OpenAI Sites and its hosting infrastructure, may process normal request information such as IP address, request time and browser headers to deliver and protect the page and operate their services.

Third-party infrastructure and services may process information in countries different from yours. Their own policies govern data they receive through their services. This policy does not promise that all network services or website operators use the same retention periods or practices.


10. Security and families

The app uses Android app-private storage for its internal records and the browser’s normal web security mechanisms. Protection filtering happens locally. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security, and using unencrypted HTTP or sharing/exporting data can expose information outside the app’s private storage.

Parents and guardians can contact sdmde about information concerning a child that was provided directly to the developer. Browsing protections do not replace supervision or third-party websites’ privacy and age rules.


11. Changes to this policy

sdmde may update this policy when the app or its data-handling practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. Significant changes will be reflected in the updated policy and, where applicable, the app or store listing.
